# Darrow

> Darrow is an AI legal-intelligence company that scans public real-world signals —
> regulatory filings, court dockets, incident reports, corporate disclosures and web
> behavior — to detect legal violations at scale, size the affected class, predict
> outcomes and value the resulting cases. Founded 2020; offices in New York, Miami,
> Arizona and Tel Aviv.

## Important for agents

Darrow publishes **no public API, no developer portal and no machine-readable API
contract**. Do not attempt to construct calls against a Darrow API from this file.

- No OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Postman collection or JSON Schema is published.
- `docs.darrow.ai`, `developers.darrow.ai`, `api.darrow.ai` do not resolve in DNS.
- The product is delivered through an Auth0-protected customer portal.
- The application backend at `https://platform.darrow.ai/api/*` returns `401 Unauthorized`
  to anonymous callers and is not a documented public interface.
- No MCP server and no A2A agent card were found on any Darrow host.
- Access is enterprise, sales-negotiated: https://www.darrow.ai/contact

## Products

- [Case Origination](https://www.darrow.ai/case-origination): surfaces litigation-ready
  opportunities early from emerging indicators of wrongdoing, with legal-merit validation.
- [Platform](https://www.darrow.ai/platform): portfolio-level case intelligence for
  plaintiff firms — active matters, estimated settlement value, projected net-to-firm,
  class size, litigation stage, defendant profile, conflict checking and case claiming.
- [Predictive Underwriting](https://www.darrow.ai/predictive-underwriting): litigation-risk
  modelling for insurers.
- [Insurance](https://www.darrow.ai/insurance) and [Law Firms](https://www.darrow.ai/law-firms):
  the two buyer-facing solution tracks.
- PlaintiffLink: plaintiff intake and campaign management, extended to mass arbitration.
- Privacy Radar: autonomous agents that compare a company's stated privacy posture with
  its live web behavior; listed in the Microsoft Marketplace (July 2026).
